The creative

Four angles,
one Sean shoot

Every ad sells MFP and sends him to Sean's video. Will's rule: test 4 to 5 ads that make different promises, not variations of one idea, then put the winners into their own ad sets and swap their openings.

Angle 1

The Blind Spot

"Every department in your company has a manager. Except one." The VSL's own opening. Makes him feel smart for seeing it, not stupid for missing it.

Angle 2

The Leftovers

The family gets what's left at the end of the day. Built from Sean's own keynote lines about walking in the door with gas left in the tank.

Angle 3

After This Busy Season

Kicking the can. "Just let me get through this season, then I'll get back in the gym." He's said it to himself, word for word.

Angle 4

Owner to Owner

"HVAC owners in Arizona." Trade and state on screen in the first two seconds. Sean talking to one kind of man, in his language.

Language to lean on: edge, standard, manager, scoreboard, run it like the business, optimize. These owners say yes to investing in themselves. They tune out "struggling", "overweight" and "unhealthy".

That also keeps every ad, the VSL and the page free of medical language, which gets ads flagged. Labs and the medical team are covered on the call.

Structure and scoreboard

How we spend,
how we judge

Structure

  • The VSL campaign optimizes for a booked call, not a click or a lead.
  • Ad sets of about $50 a day, each holding 3 to 5 ads. Budget sits at the ad set, so every ad gets a real chance.
  • Several angles across many ad sets, so no single ad set carries the budget. Will can't push one ad set much past $200 to $300 a day, even on his call funnel.
  • Total spend stays where it is now. Split it between /octconsult (control) and /vsl (test). No new money until the numbers earn it.
  • New angles are tested cheaply first: 5 ad sets at $5 a day, one ad each, open targeting, read after 3 to 5 days.

Scoreboard

  • Judge on cost per call held and closed MFP sales. Not cost per lead.
  • Read results over 3 to 7 days. Never kill or scale on one day's numbers.
  • If one ad takes the spend and is in range, leave the others alone. Pull the winner into its own ad set instead.
  • Scale in steps when an ad set stays in range for a full 3 to 5 day cycle.
