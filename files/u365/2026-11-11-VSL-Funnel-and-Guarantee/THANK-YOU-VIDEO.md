# U365 thank-you page video: Sean on camera (DRAFT)

**Plays on:** the booked page (/vsl-booked, prototype site/confirmed/), right under "You're booked."

**Job:** make him glad he booked and fired up to show up. This is a welcome, not a second pitch. He has already said yes to the call; now he has to want to keep it.

**Length:** re-time after Sean's read-through. The previous version was estimated at 50 seconds.

**Recording:** a separate take at the same shoot as the VSL. Not a cut-down of the VSL: the VSL's "Next step" section asks him to book, and on this page he already has.

**Delivery:** straight to camera, close framing, smiling, energy up. Warmer and quicker than the VSL. A phone-style selfie take works as well as the studio camera here.

**Status:** DRAFT. Sean reads it in his own words; Josh approves before it goes on any page.

## Who's on the call (read this before recording)

The MFP consult is a 60-minute Zoom on a round-robin calendar, and Alejandro takes the calls, not Sean (Josh's marketing brain, Team and Calendars sections). So the video builds excitement about working with Sean, but it never promises he'll be on the call.

## Script

ON SCREEN: You're booked.

Hey, it's Sean. You booked the call. I respect that, man. You made time to work on yourself. Now let's make sure you show up for it. `[Sean, GS26 6:08]`

ON SCREEN: What happens on the call

Come ready to tell my team what you want to work on and what's been getting in the way. We'll talk about your goals, your schedule and how the coaching works. `[Sean, podcast 11:30]` If it's not a fit, we'll tell you straight.

My team runs these calls, and it's the first step to working with me. [PENDING Josh: what the call covers]

ON SCREEN: REPLY CONFIRM · SAVE THE TIME · BRING YOUR ONE THING

Reply CONFIRM to the text, put the call on your calendar, and come ready to talk about the one thing you most want to work on.

You put it on the calendar. Now keep that commitment to yourself. I'll let my team take it from here. Let's go.

## Why it's built this way

- Opens on him, not on Sean. He just did the thing he's been putting off. Credit him for it first, so he feels like a man who keeps his word, which makes showing up the consistent thing to do.

- Explains what the conversation is for in plain words. The team discusses his goals and the coaching, then tells him honestly whether it fits.

- Gives him a clear reason to attend: talk through his goals, his schedule and how the coaching works. [CONFIRM] Josh and Alejandro must confirm the actual call deliverables. This revision does not promise a twelve-month plan on the consult.

- Keeps three actions in order: reply CONFIRM, save the time, bring one thing to discuss. Match the approved booked page before launch.

- Ends by asking him to keep the commitment he just made to himself, in the same voice as the VSL.

- Stays inside the rules: no health claims and no guarantee in Sean's words (the page carries the goal-weight guarantee line once Josh signs), and no promise that Sean takes the call.
