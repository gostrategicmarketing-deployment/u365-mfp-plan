# U365 thank-you page video: Sean on camera (DRAFT)

**Plays on:** the booked page (`/vsl-booked`, prototype `site/confirmed/`), right under "You're booked."
**Job:** make him glad he booked and fired up to show up. This is a welcome, not a second pitch. He has already said yes to the call; now he has to want to keep it.
**Length:** about 50 seconds (125 spoken words at Sean's pace of about 155 a minute).
**Recording:** a separate take at the same shoot as the VSL. Not a cut-down of the VSL: the VSL's "Next step" section asks him to book, and on this page he already has.
**Delivery:** straight to camera, close framing, smiling, energy up. Warmer and quicker than the VSL. A phone-style selfie take works as well as the studio camera here.
**Status:** DRAFT. Sean reads it in his own words; Josh approves before it goes on any page.

## Who's on the call (read this before recording)

The MFP consult is a 60-minute Zoom on a round-robin calendar, and **Alejandro takes the calls**, not Sean (Josh's marketing brain, Team and Calendars sections). So the video builds excitement about working with Sean, but it never promises he'll be on the call.

## Script

ON SCREEN: You're booked.

Hey, it's Sean. You just did something most owners keep putting off. You stopped saying "after this busy season" and put it on the calendar. I respect that, man. `[Sean, GS26 6:08]`

ON SCREEN: What happens on the call

Here's the call. We look at where you really are: your energy, your schedule, your week. Then we map out the next twelve months with somebody managing your health, the way somebody manages your business. No pressure, no crazy plan. `[Sean, podcast 11:30]` If it's not a fit, we'll tell you straight.

My team runs these calls, and it's the first step to working with me. **[PENDING Josh: what the call covers]**

ON SCREEN: Reply CONFIRM · Block the hour · Bring your one thing

Reply CONFIRM to that text, block the hour, and bring the one thing you want different in ninety days.

It's the most important meeting on your calendar this week. Show up. Let's go.

## Why it's built this way
- **Opens on him, not on Sean.** He just did the thing he's been putting off. Credit him for it first, so he feels like a man who keeps his word, which makes showing up the consistent thing to do.
- **Takes the fear out of the call.** Owners skip calls they expect to be a hard sell. "No pressure, no crazy plan, we'll tell you straight if it's not a fit" removes that reason before it forms.
- **Gives him something to look forward to:** a clear look at where he is and what the next twelve months could be, in the same "put a manager on it" language as the VSL.
- **The same three actions as the page,** in the same order (CONFIRM, block the hour, bring one thing), so the video and the page say one thing.
- **Ends on the line from the VSL** ("the most important meeting on your calendar this week"), so men who watched the VSL hear it twice.
- **Stays inside the rules:** no outcome, health or weight claims, no guarantee (the page carries that line once Josh signs), and no promise that Sean takes the call.
